## [2026-06-27 22:00] Major identity & infrastructure overhaul
### New Modules Built

**src/identity/** — Indonesian Identity System
- NIK generation (16-digit format per Permendagri): province(2) + regency(2) + district(2) + birth date(6) + serial(4)
- Female birth day +40 per regulation
- 38 provinces with kabupaten/kota, kecamatan data
- Realistic street names, RT/RW, kelurahan/desa
- KTP address + optional domisili (different address)
- Religion, marital status, occupation based on age

**src/patient/generator.ts** — Rewritten with proper ICD-10 mapping
- 40 ICD-10 codes with age/gender constraints (no male in O80, no adult in pediatrics)
- Age-appropriate vitals (pediatric vs adult vs geriatric baselines)
- Blood type, allergies, phone number
- Weighted diagnosis selection

**src/agent/** — Hospital Agent System
- 60+ agents: dokter spesialis (5), dokter umum (5), perawat (15), bidan (3), apoteker (3), etc.
- Full identity for each agent (NIK, address, religion)
- Fatigue tracking per tick, health degradation (sehat → lelah → sakit_ringan → sakit_berat)
- Shift system (pagi/siang/malam)
- Female agent physiology: menstruation cycle, pregnancy tracking
- STR/SIP/SKP license numbers

**src/referral/** — Referral System (Rujukan Berjenjang)
- 15 Puskesmas across Eastern Indonesia
- 5 Klinik
- 5 RS Tipe D, 10 RS Tipe C
- Referral letters generated every 15 ticks
- Referral pipeline: active → received → completed/returned
