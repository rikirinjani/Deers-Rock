## [2026-06-28 11:32] Research OC — First Scientific Critique

### Model Assumptions Needing Validation
- **Mortality (35/10/2%):** Plausible ranges but no source documented. High-risk 35% is defensible for sepsis in Indonesian referral hospitals. Low-risk 2% may overestimate — consider 0.5-1%.
- **LOS distribution (4-12 ticks = 4-12 simulated min):** Critically short for inpatient stays. Typical ALOS in Indonesian hospitals is 4-7 days. At 60× speed, 1 tick = 1 simulated min, so 4 ticks = 4 min simulated. Even ED observation stays are 4-6 hours (240-360 ticks). This may be the root cause of 100% bed saturation — patients leave before beds free up at realistic rates.
- **Bed saturation at 100%:** Not a bug but a system property. Admission burst (1-3 every 15 ticks = 4-12/hr) + minimum 4-tick stay creates permanent backlog. Clinically plausible for a saturated referral hospital but needs explicit documentation.
- **50% "no allergy" pool:** Pharmacy allergy detection is diluted — 3% per allergen with 50% None means only ~1.5% of patients trigger allergy checks. Hard to validate pharmacy behavior statistically.

## [2026-06-28 23:58] Research OC — E1 Experiment Data Analysis

### Platform OC delivered: seeded RNG (ADR-008), experiment runner (Milestone 2.6), 3 experiment runs

### E1 Multi-Run (10×1000 ticks, 50 patients) — Key Findings

**1. Deaths = exactly 10/10 runs — zero variance (critical signal)**
This uniform death count strongly suggests the morgue capacity of 10 is a hard ceiling, not a recording limit. The death roll fires but once the morgue hits 10, `newMorgue.length < state.morgueCapacity` (markov.ts:111) blocks further death recording. Constitution §3.4 says overflow "shall be silently accepted" — but the code silently drops overflows from the record. This makes mortality statistics untrustworthy beyond 10 deaths. **Root cause identified: Constitution vs code contradiction on morgue capacity semantics.**

**2. avgLosTicks = 7 in all 10 runs** (first experiment run had a calculation bug showing raw ms instead of minutes). Confirms earlier finding: ALOS of ~7 simulated minutes is unrealistically short for inpatient admissions.

**3. All order types at pruning caps across all 10 runs** — 200 lab, 200 meds, 50 surgery, 100 charges. The system is running at constitutional collection limits, not clinical demand. Pruning is the dominant constraint.

**4. Disaster events fire in 3/10 runs but mortality is identical (10 deaths).** Scenario mortality boosts (+8-15%) produce no observable effect — hidden by the morgue ceiling. The scenario system's mortality signal is invisible at current configuration.

**5. Peak bed occupancy: 6-11 out of 95 beds (~10%).** Not saturated — contradicting earlier concern. The 50-patient pool cycles through in ~7 min each, so few are in the hospital at any moment.
