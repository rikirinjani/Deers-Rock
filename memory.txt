**src/identity/** — Indonesian Identity System

- 38 provinces with kabupaten/kota, kecamatan data
- Realistic street names, RT/RW, kelurahan/desa

**src/patient/generator.ts** — Rewritten with proper ICD-10 mapping
- 40 ICD-10 codes with age/gender constraints (no male in O80, no adult in pediatrics)
