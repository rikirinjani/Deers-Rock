## [2026-06-28 11:32] Research OC — First Scientific Critique

### Model Assumptions Needing Validation
- **Mortality (35/10/2%):** Plausible ranges but no source documented. High-risk 35% is defensible for sepsis in Indonesian referral hospitals. Low-risk 2% may overestimate — consider 0.5-1%.
- **LOS distribution (4-12 ticks = 4-12 simulated min):** Critically short for inpatient stays. Typical ALOS in Indonesian hospitals is 4-7 days. At 60× speed, 1 tick = 1 simulated min, so 4 ticks = 4 min simulated. Even ED observation stays are 4-6 hours (240-360 ticks). This may be the root cause of 100% bed saturation — patients leave before beds free up at realistic rates.
- **Bed saturation at 100%:** Not a bug but a system property. Admission burst (1-3 every 15 ticks = 4-12/hr) + minimum 4-tick stay creates permanent backlog. Clinically plausible for a saturated referral hospital but needs explicit documentation.
- **50% "no allergy" pool:** Pharmacy allergy detection is diluted — 3% per allergen with 50% None means only ~1.5% of patients trigger allergy checks. Hard to validate pharmacy behavior statistically.
