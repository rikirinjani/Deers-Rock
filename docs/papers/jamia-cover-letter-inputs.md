- **Manuscript title:** "Deers Rock: A Deterministic Micro-Simulation of a Tier A Referral Hospital in Eastern Indonesia"
- **Article type:** Research and Applications (JAMIA)
- **Contribution (one paragraph):** Deers Rock is an open-source, deterministic micro-simulation of a Tier A referral hospital in Eastern Indonesia. It combines a deterministic tick engine, a bounded SQLite event journal with snapshot/replay, and a modular chain of 38 domain handlers; it models nine clinical departments, 49 unique ICD-10 diagnoses, a 22-drug formulary, seven disaster types, and culturally contextualized patient generation. Across 10 seeded runs of 1,000 ticks it produced consistent, reproducible outcomes (mean LOS 82.9 ± 10.4; deaths 4.3 ± 1.9; mean peak bed occupancy 130.9 of 133 beds, 98%). All clinical parameters are plausibility-based and uncalibrated; the contribution is architectural transparency and reproducibility, not clinical validity.
