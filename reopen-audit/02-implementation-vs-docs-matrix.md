## Summary of matrix

- **Verified TRUE:** 1 (cross-process determinism), 2 (mulberry32), 4 (tick=minute), 10 (seed derivation), 11 (RNG independence), 12 (boundary isolation), 13 (sentinel independence), 22 (snapshot).
- **Partial/misleading:** 3 (determinism untested at scale), 5 (1440 vs 10 ticks), 8 (sentinel fields partially real), 19 (significance flags degenerate), 20 (power), 23 (scenario), 25 (soft violation), 28/29 (doc drift).
- **FALSE / not implemented / unreproducible:** 6, 7 (adapter inert), 9 (dead reverse channel), 14, 15 (P-004 unreproducible, effects are artifacts), 16 (rewind aliasing bug), 17, 18 (statistics invalid), 21 (fastForward absent), 24 (stale test claims), 26 (no calibration), 27 (t+1 false), 30 (superseded numbers).

**Bottom line:** the architectural core (deterministic, seed-isolated, boundary-clean micro-simulation) is REAL and VERIFIED. The integration story (adapter coupling, sentinel feedback, counterfactual results) is NOT supported by the current implementation.
